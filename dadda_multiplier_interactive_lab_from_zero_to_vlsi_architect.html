Step 1 of 4 • Zero Prior Knowledge Needed

What problem does a Dadda Multiplier solve?

When a computer chip multiplies two N-bit binary numbers, generating the grid of partial products is super fast (just a single layer of AND gates). The bottleneck is adding all those rows together.

In 1965, Italian computer scientist Luigi Dadda asked: "How can we squish a tall pile of partial-product bits down to just 2 rows using the absolute minimum number of adders and the fewest possible delay stages?"

1

1 Dot = 1 Bit

Instead of writing cluttered a₂·b₁ labels everywhere, Dadda represents each partial-product bit as a single dot in its power-of-2 column (2k).

2

Adders Are "Dot Squishers"

A Full Adder (3:2 compressor) eats 3 dots in column k and outputs 1 Sum dot in column k + 1 Carry dot in column k+1. Net height in column k drops by 2!

3

Stop at Height = 2

Once every column has at most 2 dots, we have just two binary numbers left—which a single fast Carry-Propagate Adder (CPA) sums at the end.

The Magic Target Height Sequence (dj)

Work backward from the end! Before the final Carry-Propagate Adder, we want at most d1 = 2 dots per column. Since each Full Adder reduces 3 dots into 2 dots (1 Sum + 1 Carry), working backward each earlier stage can be at most ⌊1.5 × dj⌋ tall:

How to Reduce Any Stage (Target Height = d)

Scan columns from right to left (k = 0, 1, 2, …). For column k, count its total height H = (dots currently in col k) + (carries arriving from col k−1 in this stage):

Case 1: H ≤ d
Do Nothing!

The column is already at or below the stage limit d. Save hardware and pass all dots untouched to the next stage.

Case 2: H − d ≥ 2
Add a Full Adder (FA)

Each FA reduces H by 2 (and sends +1 carry to col k+1). Repeat while H − d ≥ 2.

Case 3: H − d == 1
Add 1 Half Adder (HA)

We only need to shave off 1 dot so H lands right on d (and sends +1 carry to col k+1).

Why Do Chip Designers Prefer Dadda Over Wallace Trees?

Both Wallace (1964) and Dadda (1965) reduce partial products in logarithmic O(log1.5 N) stages. However, Wallace greedily compresses every group of 3 or 2 dots as early as possible, whereas Dadda compresses only enough to meet the target ceiling dj. Compare their exact hardware synthesis below!
